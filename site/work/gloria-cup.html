The challenge.

Located on a rooftop opposite Emporium Mall in Johar Town, Lahore, Gloria Cup Café needed more than static menu posts—it needed to capture the energy of live cricket match screenings, sizzling Desperado Steaks, loaded pizza platters, and evening cappuccinos so local foodies would choose it as their go-to hangout.

Strategic direction.

I architected a lifestyle- and food-first social engine for @gloriacupcafe built around high-retention rooftop Reels, food creator collaborations, deal-driven carousels, and match-day screening promos that turned online views into packed rooftop tables.

Execution in motion.

  • Positioned the rooftop view (opposite Emporium Mall) and retro street-art ambiance as a signature visual backdrop across Reels and Stories.
  • Engineered repeatable short-form Reel formats—cheese-pull pizza close-ups, Desperado Steak sizzles, cappuccino pours, and budget platter reveals.
  • Partnered with local Lahore food creators and vloggers to showcase group deals and drive high-trust word-of-mouth discovery.
  • Built event-driven urgency around live cricket tournament screenings and late-night (2 PM – 12 AM) hangout deals via Instagram and Foodpanda.

Commercial impact.

Scaled the brand’s organic social footprint past 20,000+ engaged local foodies across platforms, driving consistent daily DM/WhatsApp reservations, viral platter Reels, and packed rooftop match screenings.

Deliverables & stack.

Core production capabilities and deliverables executed in this workflow:

  • End-to-End Instagram Management & Grid Direction (@gloriacupcafe)
  • On-Site Rooftop Reel Production, Creator Collabs & Hook Scripting
  • Promotional Deal Graphics, Platter Campaigns & Live Screening Event Push
  • Community Management, Story Repost Loops & Local Discovery SEO
