The challenge.

High-ticket luxury furniture—from hand-carved 8- and 10-seater royal dining sets to bouclé bedroom suites—never sells on specifications alone. Buyers need to feel the craftsmanship, texture, and room presence before they ever message on WhatsApp or visit the PIA Road showroom.

Strategic direction.

I elevated @furniturecorner.pk into a lavish digital showroom—combining editorial interior staging, close-up texture Reels, and full-funnel Meta Business Suite catalog campaigns engineered to convert high-intent homeowners and interior buyers.

Execution in motion.

  • Directed lavish, warm-lit room staging for flagship collections: Royal Gold & Marble Dining Sets, Bouclé Bubble Bed Sets, and Modern Luxe LED Consoles.
  • Produced short-form vertical Showroom Reels that pan across wood carving details, velvet/bouclé textures, and full bedroom/dining setups.
  • Structured targeted Meta Business Suite ad campaigns reaching active homeowners, bridal/dowry shoppers, and interior design enthusiasts.
  • Streamlined the Instagram-to-WhatsApp (0309-9566665) inquiry funnel to turn Reel views and post saves into showroom visits and nationwide orders.

Commercial impact.

Drove a +40% year-over-year commercial revenue expansion across 290+ curated Instagram posts and Reels, significantly lowering cost-per-inquiry on Meta placements and increasing high-ticket dining and bedroom set orders.

Deliverables & stack.

Core production capabilities and deliverables executed in this workflow:

  • Lavish Digital Catalog & Instagram Grid Curation (@furniturecorner.pk)
  • Short-Form Showroom Reels (Bouclé Bed Sets, Royal Dining & Lounge Collections)
  • Meta Business Suite Ad Architecture, Retargeting & Audience Segmentation
  • Direct-Response Copywriting & WhatsApp/Showroom Conversion Funnel
