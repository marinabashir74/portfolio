Furniture Corner.

Transforming static furniture listings into high-converting visual stories and +40% year-over-year commercial growth.

Client / ShowcaseFurniture Corner (Retail & E-Commerce)
RolePerformance Content Strategist & Meta Campaign Lead
Core Stack & ScopeVisual Catalogs · Meta Ads · Conversion Copy
Key Highlight+40% YoY Revenue Expansion

The challenge.

High-consideration home furniture never sells on specifications alone. Showing a chair or sofa in isolation is one thing; helping a buyer immediately picture how it transforms their living space—and giving them the confidence to inquire—is what drives revenue.

Strategic direction.

I bridged the gap between editorial interior inspiration and direct-response commerce—rebuilding Furniture Corner’s visual catalog system and pairing it with targeted Meta Business Suite campaigns engineered to convert high-intent home buyers.

Execution in motion.

  • Redesigned product creative around real buyer decisions—room styling, dimensions, material texture, and spatial flow.
  • Built scroll-stopping multi-slide carousels and staged video features that work seamlessly across organic feeds and paid placements.
  • Structured full-funnel Meta Business Suite ad campaigns targeting active homeowners, movers, and interior shoppers.
  • Optimized direct-response copy and WhatsApp/DM inquiry handoffs to shorten the path from first click to closed sale.

Commercial impact.

Drove a +40% year-over-year commercial revenue expansion through stronger catalog conversion rates, lower cost-per-inquiry on Meta placements, and higher-intent showroom and online orders.

Deliverables & stack.

Core production capabilities and deliverables executed in this workflow:

  • Conversion-Engineered Visual Product Catalogs & Carousel Systems
  • Meta Business Suite Campaign Setup, Targeting & Creative A/B Testing
  • Direct-Response Sales Copywriting & WhatsApp/DM Inquiry Funnel
  • Monthly Commercial Attribution & Merchandising Optimization
