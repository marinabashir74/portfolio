### Compression & performance

- Serve images at 1x/2x device pixel ratio, and generate 480/800/1200/1600 wide variants for responsive photographs.
- Put fixed `width` and `height` attributes on images; set `loading="lazy"` for below-fold media.
- Prioritize the first hero image and make posters purposeful, not meaningless decoration.
- Use AVIF or WebP for photos, SVG for simple vectors, and compressed MP4 for short clips.
- Don't auto-play audio; offer captions/transcripts and clearly labeled playback controls.
- Record permission and attribution for every external clip, photo and logo.
- Run a performance audit after genuine media is added. The current concept's small local SVG assets will not predict the speed of the real video-heavy site.
